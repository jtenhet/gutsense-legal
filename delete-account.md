# Delete your GutSense account

GutSense lets you permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time, free of charge.

## How to delete your account

### In the app

1. Open **GutSense** on your phone.
2. Tap **Settings** in the bottom tab bar.
3. Scroll to the **Data** section.
4. Tap **Delete account** and confirm.

### By email

If you no longer have the app installed, email **info@tenhetapp.com** from the address associated with your account and ask us to delete it. We will process the request within 7 days and confirm by reply.

## What is deleted

When you delete your account, the following is **permanently removed**:

- Your account (email and authentication credentials)
- All meal entries
- All symptom entries
- All meal photos
- All computed pattern insights
- Your profile (display name, known allergens, dietary restrictions)

If you have an active GutSense Plus subscription, it must be cancelled separately through your Apple App Store or Google Play account — Apple and Google control that billing, not us.

## What is retained

Nothing personal. GutSense does not retain personal data after account deletion. Anonymous, aggregated usage events (such as the daily count of active sessions) are not linked to you and cannot be used to identify you.

## Questions

Contact **info@tenhetapp.com** with any question about the deletion process.
